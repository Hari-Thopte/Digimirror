- **14:** Generic guidance is retained for fictional services and the cross-service advertising action. Those services have no real interface to verify. Google Timeline guidance was checked against official help; the flow never claims to change an account.
- **32:** Landing and Settings are static information/preferences screens. They have loading/error/recovery and normal states, but no fabricated data-empty view. Data-bearing screens have real clear-data empty states and export guidance.
- **58:** Print summary and print stylesheet are implemented; native browser print/PDF output has not been visually inspected.

Hindi copy was not invented: the locale contract and expansion fixture are ready, with Hindi explicitly pending translation review. No backend, login, live scan, monitoring or real account-setting change was added. Public deployment and social-card scraping were outside this local frontend request.

## Three weakest screens

1. **Exposure map:** four sample services do not exercise a dense graph. Next, test consented real-size export structures and node collisions.
2. **Privacy plan:** generic fictional-service steps are the weakest point. Next, choose supported providers and maintain verified, versioned walkthroughs.
3. **Lab:** useful grouping, but still illustrative tool dialogs. Next, test which exploration helps people choose a setting before expanding its visual treatment.
